# Privacy Policy

Tweetmoder has no server and collects no data or personal information. Its settings are stored in your browser.

Reply trees, quoted tweets, Quote Tweets, translations, likes and replies are loaded through X's own website, using the session you are already signed in with, the same way X's own pages load them.

**Ask about this tweet** is the only feature that sends anything elsewhere. When you click its **?** button, the tweet you asked about, its quoted tweet, link card, images, some of the conversation around it, and your follow-up questions are sent to the AI provider you chose in the options:

- **Codex (local helper):** a program on your computer runs OpenAI's Codex, which sends them to OpenAI under your Codex account.
- **OpenAI API:** sent to OpenAI with your API key.
- **OpenAI-compatible API:** sent to the API URL you entered.

Nothing is sent until you click **?**, and you can turn the feature off in the options. Your API key stays in the extension's storage and is only sent to the provider it belongs to.
